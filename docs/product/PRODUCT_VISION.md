# Product Vision

## What this is
A **Career & Employment Intelligence Operating System**: four products sharing one
governed platform core, serving every side of the employment market — the person
looking for work, the organisation hiring, the publicly-funded service helping
people into work, and the person changing career.

## Why one platform, not four products
The same objects appear in all four. A candidate's structured career profile is
what a recruiter shortlists, what a case manager builds an action plan around, and
what a career consultant runs a transition analysis against. An occupation, a
skill, a credential and a job posting mean the same thing in every product.

Building four applications would mean four copies of that data, four matching
engines, four consent models and four security boundaries. The platform exists so
these are defined once, governed once, and reused.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Truthfulness before capability.** AI may prioritise, reframe, summarise,
   reorganise and truthfully adapt terminology. It may never invent employment,
   responsibilities, technologies, years of experience, revenue, achievements,
   education, certifications, clients or projects. Enforced structurally by the
   Career Evidence Vault, not by prompt wording.
2. **Transparency over scores.** Every compatibility number decomposes into named
   dimensions with cited evidence. No `resume + JD → LLM → percentage`.
3. **Eligibility is not fit.** Hard gates — work authorisation, licensure,
   location — are evaluated first, separately, with reasons. Nobody is shown a
   92% match for a job they cannot legally hold.
4. **The candidate owns their data.** Consent is explicit, granular and revocable.
   No employer sees a candidate who has not consented to that disclosure.
5. **Humans decide.** Applications are prepared, not sent autonomously. The
   case-manager copilot recommends; the case manager decides.
6. **Lawful acquisition only.** No CAPTCHA bypass, no access-control
   circumvention, no fingerprint evasion. Ever.
7. **Sensitive attributes never influence outcomes.** Demographic
   self-identification is architecturally segregated from every decision path.
8. **Canada first, properly.** NOC/TEER, economic regions, CAD, bilingual-ready —
   with US support coexisting rather than overwriting Canadian semantics.
9. **The founder can operate the business.** Routine business change happens in
   an admin interface, not a code deploy.
10. **Honest status.** A mock is never described as production. An unvalidated
    integration is never called validated.

## What the product must eventually answer

| For | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate | Which real jobs am I eligible for, how well do I fit, what is missing, and what exactly was sent on my behalf? |
| Employer / recruiter | Who is genuinely qualified, consented and available — and how is my pipeline performing? |
| Case manager | Which of my clients is stuck, why, and what intervention would help? |
| Career changer | **Will this course or certification materially improve my eligibility for the jobs I want?** |
| Founder | What is happening in the business, and can I change it myself? |

The fourth is the hardest and the most differentiating. It requires the
occupation graph, the skills taxonomy, the eligibility engine and the learning
catalog to be one connected, computable model — which is why it is architecture,
not content.
